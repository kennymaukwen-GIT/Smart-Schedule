9. 出發提醒

  • 你告訴助理你要在什麼時間到哪裡，或問某個行程什麼時候該出發時，App 可以算出你該什麼時候出發，並設定兩個提醒：一個提醒你準備出門，一個在該出發時提醒你。
  • 交通時間是 Apple 的地圖服務估算的，由你的 iPhone 直接向它查詢，不經過我們的伺服器或 Gemini（傳送哪些內容，見隱私權政策第 8 節）。實際的交通、大眾運輸和路況可能不同。建議的時間已經提早 10 分鐘作為緩衝，但重要的事請預留更多時間。
  • 提醒上的「出發」按鈕會打開 Google 地圖，只帶入目的地原本的文字（你輸入的，或行程的地點）。Google 地圖可能找到不同的地點或路線，所以請確認目的地。Google 地圖適用 Google 的條款。
  • 有時 App 算不出時間，例如沒有位置權限、從 Siri 或通知回覆、地點太遠，或找不到路線。這時你要求的行程仍會加入，但不會設定出發提醒，回覆會說明。沒有位置權限時，助理可能改問你從哪裡出發。
  • 開車時絕對不要操作 App。請在出發前設定好，並遵守交通法規。

7. The AI assistant

  • The assistant is optional. It uses Google's Gemini API through our server, which runs on Cloudflare. 9. Departure reminders

  • When you tell the assistant where you need to be and when, or ask when to leave for an event, the app can work out when you should leave and set two reminders: one to get ready, and one when it is time to leave.
  • The travel time is an estimate from Apple's map service. Your iPhone asks it directly; our server and Gemini are not involved (for what is sent, see section 8 of the Privacy Policy). Real traffic, public transport and road conditions can differ. The suggested time already includes a 10-minute margin, but for anything important, leave extra time.
  • The reminder's button for setting off opens Google Maps with only the destination as it was written (what you typed, or the event's location). Google Maps may find a different place or route, so check the destination. Google's terms apply to Google Maps.
  • Sometimes the app can't work out a time, for example without location permission, from Siri or a notification reply, when the place is too far away, or when no route is found. Then any event you asked for is still added, but no departure reminders are set, and the reply says so. Without location permission, the assistant may ask where you are leaving from instead.
  • Never interact with the app while driving. Set things up before you leave, and follow traffic laws.
