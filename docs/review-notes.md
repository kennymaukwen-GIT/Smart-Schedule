# App Review notes（背景說明，不要整份貼上）

給審核員的說明的背景版本，比 App Store Connect 的 Notes 上限（4000 位元組）長，也沒有測試帳號和備用驗證碼。**要貼進 App Store Connect › App Review Information › Notes 的是 [`app-store-metadata.md`](app-store-metadata.md) 第 8.2 節的 `asc:en/review-notes` 區塊**（目前 3,899 位元組，剩 101 位元組；填完備用驗證碼後用 `app-store-metadata.md` 第 12 節的腳本重算）；審核員追問細節時，可以從下面的英文段落或第 8.3 節摘取。最後一節是送審前自己要確認的事項。

---

**How to review without a Google account.** On the first screen tap "Try without signing in". Demo mode has fictional sample calendars and needs no Google sign-in. Its assistant is the real one: after the "Use the AI assistant?" dialog it sends what the reviewer types and the sample events to Google's Gemini API (never Google data); without permission, or when the AI can't be reached, offline demo rules answer and the reply says so. Every feature below can be tried.

**Live Activities / Dynamic Island.** Smart Schedules shows a Live Activity only for an event that is about to start or under way:

- Each timed event gets its own Live Activity. It appears 30, 15, 10 or 5 minutes before the event starts (Settings › Notifications › Dynamic Island; default 30 minutes, or Off). The app ends it in its first update after the event ends (when the app comes to the front, after a change to the events, or in a background refresh): it leaves the Dynamic Island at once and stays on the Lock Screen, marked "Ended", until 10 minutes after the event's end, or leaves at once if that time has passed. Nothing ends it on time while the app does not run: iOS then ends it 8 hours after it appeared and can keep it on the Lock Screen for up to 4 more hours unless the person removes it. No server is involved.
- When no event is coming up, there is no Live Activity. The app never keeps one open as a permanent widget.
- iOS 26: the app schedules each activity ahead with `Activity.request(…, start:)` for events in the next 48 hours, with the start alert iOS requires, so it appears on time even when the app is closed. iOS 18 cannot schedule an activity ahead, and version 1.0 does not start one from a server, so on iOS 18 the activity appears when the app is opened during the lead time or while the event is under way, and stays after the app is closed.
- Everything happens on the iPhone, on both versions: Live Activities need no AI data sharing, and no event details are sent to our server for them.
- The countdown uses `Text(timerInterval:)`, so the activity needs no updates from a server. `NSSupportsLiveActivitiesFrequentUpdates` is declared, but the app receives no updates from a server at all.
- Tapping the Live Activity opens the app's quick-input screen (`smartschedule://compose`). In the expanded Dynamic Island, the Edit button opens the same screen with the keyboard up.

**Control Center control.** The "Smart Schedules" control opens the app directly on the quick-input screen with the keyboard up (an `OpenIntent`). It does not start a Live Activity.

**Time Sensitive notifications.** Reminders are Time Sensitive only when they fire 30 minutes or less before an event starts (the default "Automatic" urgency). Earlier reminders, the morning briefing and the evening preview are Normal, and a long-term plan's daily nudge arrives silently. People can change the urgency per kind of event in Settings › Notifications › Urgency, including turning Time Sensitive off. During quiet hours reminders arrive silently.

**Notification permission.** Asked the first time there is something to remind about: when "Try without signing in" (or ☰ › General › Demo mode) starts the sample calendar, before it loads, so its reminders are scheduled after the answer, or after connecting a Google account; never at first launch. Live Activities have their own iOS setting and do not need it.

**Long-press on a reminder.** The notification content extension lets people reply to a reminder by typing (or with the keyboard's dictation). The extension calls the AI service (through our server) and Google Calendar itself, so a change ("move it to 4 pm") or a deletion is done before the notification closes. As in the chat and Quick Capture, one event is deleted at once; several events, every occurrence of a repeating event or a long-term plan wait for a red "Confirm delete" button (demo mode's offline rules always ask). With "Ask me first" on, new events wait on a card with Add; changes and single deletions are not held. On a locked iPhone, replying asks for Face ID or the passcode first, and the card's own field and buttons (Add and "Confirm delete" included) change nothing while it is locked; "Snooze 10 min" only schedules a local notification and works while locked. The extension uses an ephemeral network session with a 30-second timeout.

**AI and personal data (Guideline 5.1.2(i)).** Before anything is sent, a one-time dialog lists exactly what goes to Google's Gemini API: what the person types or says (spoken requests as text: Siri and the keyboard's dictation are Apple's, and the app's microphone button turns speech into text on the iPhone), the titles, times, notes and Google event IDs of up to 40 events in the next 14 days and of the event whose reminder they reply to, the location of one event only when they ask for AI suggestions on it, their calendar names and assistant settings, and their time zone and language. Google account email addresses are never sent: the AI sees calendars as "Account 1", "Account 2", and the app removes the accounts' addresses from event text and error messages first. The dialog names Google as the recipient, says requests pass through our server, which does not keep them, and asks for permission ("Allow" / "Not now"). The same dialog appears when permission is given later in Settings › AI & privacy. Nothing is sent to the AI before the person taps Allow. Permission can be withdrawn in Settings › AI & privacy. Demo mode asks the same way; with permission its requests carry the fictional sample events instead of Google data, and without it offline rules answer. The wording in 1.0 is version 7 of the disclosure, so people who allowed an earlier version, including TestFlight testers, are asked again.

**AI content (Guidelines 1.2, 4.7.1).** The assistant only handles calendar and time requests: adding, moving, changing, deleting and finding events, reminders and the app's own settings, when to leave, dates, times, time zones and durations, plans for goals that become calendar events, and how to use the app. It politely declines anything else (general knowledge, homework, writing or translating, code, opinions, the weather or news, medical, legal or financial advice, and anything harmful or explicit) in one short sentence with an example of what it can do, and never answers the off-topic part; scheduling stays fine whatever the subject ("remind me to take my medicine at 9"). This rule is the `# Scope` section of the system prompt, sent with every assistant (chat) request: the chat, Quick Capture, Siri and Shortcuts, and replies typed in a notification. AI suggestions for one event have their own prompt, which only suggests things to bring, prepare or check and gives no medical, legal or financial advice. Gemini's safety filters are on for every request, AI suggestions included: harassment, hate speech and sexually explicit content are blocked from medium probability up (`BLOCK_MEDIUM_AND_ABOVE`), dangerous content only at high probability (`BLOCK_ONLY_HIGH`), because each request carries the person's own event titles and notes and ordinary ones (a chemistry exam, a shooting range, picking up a prescription) must not make requests fail. When Gemini blocks a request or an answer, the reply is "I can't help with that. I can help with your events, times and plans." (AI suggestions show the app's own suggestions instead). Other failed answers from Gemini, and our server's general errors, show "Something went wrong with that request. Please try again, or say it another way.", or "The assistant is busy right now. Please try again in a moment." when either is overloaded; the server's limits keep their own messages ("Today's limit is used up…"). Never Gemini's raw reason or Google's own message, and Siri speaks the same text. To report a reply, people touch and hold an assistant reply, or a card the assistant made (a plan, the events it added), in the chat or Quick Capture and choose Report this reply (next to Copy; VoiceOver offers both as actions). A dialog offers Open Email, which copies the report and opens a new email to support@smart-schedules.com, and Copy Report, which only copies it, so the report can always be pasted into any email app. The email has the subject "Report an AI reply" and quotes the reply, what its cards show (which can name the person's events, with their times and places; they can edit or delete that before sending) and its suggested replies, never the request that led to it, with the app and iOS versions and a line for saying what was wrong. Replies from Siri or a notification, and AI suggestions on an event page, are reported from Settings › AI & privacy › Report an AI reply: the same dialog, titled "Report an AI reply", and the email asks the person to paste or describe the reply. The developer reads every report and answers by email, usually within 3 business days; the same address is on the support page and in Settings › About › Contact support.

**Sign in with Apple (Guideline 4.8).** Not required. The app has no account of its own. Connecting a Google account ("Connect Google Calendar" / "Add Google account") only authorizes access to the person's Google Calendar (Calendar scopes), the same way a calendar app connects to a calendar service. No app account is created and no login is needed to use the app (see demo mode).

**Background modes.** Only `fetch`: background app refresh syncs Google Calendar changes and keeps reminders and Live Activities up to date. Reminders are local notifications created on the iPhone.

**Microphone and speech recognition.** Used only for the microphone button next to the text field in the chat and Quick Capture, and only after the person taps it; both permissions are asked on that first tap. Speech is recognised on the iPhone only (`SFSpeechAudioBufferRecognitionRequest.requiresOnDeviceRecognition = true`, so it also works in Airplane Mode); the button is hidden when the iPhone can't recognise the chosen language (Chinese (Taiwan) or English (US)) on the device. The audio is not saved or sent anywhere: the words fill the text field, and nothing is sent until the person taps Send. Recording stops on a second tap, on Send, after a few seconds of silence, after 60 seconds, and when the screen closes or something covers it. The widget and notification extensions have no microphone. Voice input is free.

**Location.** Version 1.0 does not use location: no location permission, no Core Location and no MapKit.

**In-app purchases.** Version 1.0 has no in-app purchases or subscriptions; every feature is free to use. The binary contains StoreKit code that is switched off at build time; it never runs in 1.0 and can only be turned on in a later version submitted for review.

**AI limits.** To prevent abuse (nothing is sold), each installation (its App Attest key) can make 30 AI requests a minute, and replies typed in a notification can use up to 200 AI requests a day (UTC; one reply uses 1 to 3); each network address can also register only a limited number of new installations a day. When a daily limit is reached the app says "Today's limit is used up. Try again tomorrow." (in a notification: "Today's limit for replies here is used up. Open Smart Schedules to continue, or try again tomorrow."). The daily counts are deleted after 2 days.

**Age rating.** The app is rated 18+ because the AI service it uses, Google's Gemini API, is only for people 18 or older.
