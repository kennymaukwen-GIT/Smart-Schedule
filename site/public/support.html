支援

Smart Schedules for iPhone · English version

聯絡我們

有問題、建議或隱私相關的請求，請寄信到 support@smart-schedules.com。

為了更快幫你，請附上：

  • iPhone 機型和 iOS 版本；
  • Smart Schedules 的版本（設定 › 關於 › 版本）；
  • 發生了什麼、你預期會怎樣，以及重現的步驟（截圖很有幫助）。

我們看不到你的行事曆、聊天記錄或裝置上的任何資料，所以請用文字說明。請不要寄密碼、驗證碼或登入權杖，我們絕不會向你要這些。

多久會回覆

每一封信我們都會讀，通常在 3 個工作天內回覆。隱私相關的請求（主旨請註明「隱私」）會在法律規定的期限內處理。

設定的位置：點左上角的 ☰ › 一般。

怎麼刪除我的資料？

到 設定 › 資料 › 清除這台裝置上的所有資料。這會：

  • 停止即時動態、移除提醒通知；
  • 撤回 AI 資料分享；
  • 向 Google 要求撤銷授權（需要網路連線），並刪除所有登入權杖；
  • 刪除帳號、快取的行程、待送出的變更、設定、聊天記錄和匯出檔。

Google 日曆裡的行程不會被刪除；要刪除行程本身，請在 Google 日曆裡刪除。我們伺服器上剩下的少量技術資料不含行程內容，大部分會在幾天內到期；訂閱記錄會在伺服器收到的最晚到期日過後 30 天刪除（見隱私權政策的保存期限）。清除資料不會取消訂閱，取消方法見下方的訂閱問答。

打算刪除 App 的話，請在有網路時先清除資料：刪除 App 不會撤銷 Google 授權，iOS 也可能保留登入權杖。清除後，可以到 Google 帳號的第三方應用程式和服務確認 Smart Schedules 已經不在清單上。

怎麼移除 Google 帳號？

點右上角的頭像，在「Google 帳號」裡把那個帳號向左滑（或點「⋯」），選「移除帳號」。也可以在 ☰ 選單裡點帳號 email 旁的「⋯」。移除時，App 會向 Google 要求撤銷 Smart Schedules 的授權，並從這支 iPhone 刪除該帳號的登入權杖和快取的行程；Google 日曆裡的資料不會被刪除。如果還有變更沒同步到 Google，App 會先提醒你，並提供「先同步」。

撤銷授權需要網路連線。如果移除時沒有網路，或你想確認授權已經撤銷，請到 Google 帳號的第三方應用程式和服務移除 Smart Schedules 的存取權。

怎麼停止 AI 資料分享（撤回同意）？

到 設定 › AI 與隱私 › 停止分享。之後助理不會再傳送任何內容給 Google Gemini。動態島和即時動態不受影響：它們本來就在你的 iPhone 上產生，不需要 AI 資料分享。示範模式仍然可以使用，改用離線規則。想再開啟時，到同一頁按「允許」。同一頁的「傳送的內容」列出會傳送哪幾類資料（例如「你的行事曆名稱和助理設定，但不包括你 Google 帳號的 email」）；完整的傳送內容請看隱私權政策的第 4 節。

動態島什麼時候會出現？

每個有時間的行程，會在開始前你設定的時間出現在動態島和鎖定畫面（設定 › 通知 › 動態島 › 顯示下一個行程：30、15、10 或 5 分鐘，預設 30 分鐘），行程結束後消失。即時動態直接在你的 iPhone 上產生，不需要允許 AI 資料分享，也不會把行程傳到我們的伺服器。

  • iOS 26 以上：每次打開 Smart Schedules，App 會安排接下來兩天的即時動態；時間到了，即使沒有打開 App 也會準時出現。
  • iOS 18：iOS 18 不讓 App 預先安排，所以只有在行程開始前的這段時間內或行程進行中打開 Smart Schedules，行程才會出現；出現之後，關掉 App 它也會留著。

一直沒有出現的話，確認「顯示下一個行程」不是「關閉」，以及 iPhone 設定 › App › Smart Schedules 裡的「即時動態」是開著的。詳細說明見隱私權政策的第 5 節。

有 App 內購買或訂閱嗎？

有。AI 助理每月有少量免費的文字請求額度（打字，或用說的）。透過 Apple 在 App 內購買的自動續訂訂閱，提供較多的每月額度，並可以附加照片和 PDF；另有額度較少的免費試用。訂閱前，App 會顯示價格和內容。

  • 付款由 Apple 處理，我們不會收到你的付款資料，也不會收到你 Apple 帳號的姓名或 email。
  • 取消訂閱：iPhone 設定 › 你的名字 › 訂閱項目。刪除 App 或清除資料不會取消訂閱。
  • 訂閱時，伺服器只保留 Apple 為訂閱指定的原始交易 ID、到期日和本月（以及試用期間）的 AI 請求次數，在它收到的最晚到期日過後 30 天刪除；免費額度只在這個安裝的 App Attest 記錄裡計算當月的請求次數。詳見隱私權政策的第 7 節。

AI 助理需要什麼才能運作？

  • iOS 18 以上的 iPhone 和網路連線。
  • 允許 AI 資料分享（第一次使用時的說明，或 設定 › AI 與隱私 › 允許）。
  • 連接 Google 帳號，助理才能讀寫你的行事曆。不登入的話，示範模式會用虛構的範例行事曆；允許 AI 資料分享後可以試用真正的 AI 助理，否則用離線規則。
  • 年滿 18 歲：Smart Schedules 只提供給年滿 18 歲的人使用（App Store 分級 18+），這是 Google Gemini API 條款的規定。
  • 還有每月額度：免費使用者每月有少量額度，訂閱有較多額度（見上方的訂閱問答）。
  • 想用說的：在對話或快速輸入點麥克風按鈕，iOS 詢問時允許語音辨識和麥克風。語音在你的 iPhone 上轉成文字，聲音不會被錄下或傳送到任何地方；你按送出之前，什麼都不會傳送。沒有看到麥克風按鈕，表示這支 iPhone 還不能在裝置上辨識這個語言；鍵盤的聽寫鍵和 Siri 仍然可以用。
  • 附加照片或 PDF：需要訂閱。「拍照」會詢問相機權限；從照片選擇器選照片或從「檔案」選 PDF 不需要權限，App 只會收到你選的那一個。找到的行程會先給你確認，按「加入」才會加入。

助理會傳送什麼，請看隱私權政策的 AI 助理一節。

怎麼匯出我的資料？

到 設定 › 資料 › 匯出我的資料。App 會產生一個 JSON 檔案，包含設定、助理記得的事、帳號和行事曆、快取的行程和聊天記錄，用分享選單存到你想要的地方。檔案不含登入權杖。你附加過的照片和 PDF 不會保留，所以也不在匯出檔裡。

為什麼 Google 日曆的行程說明出現「由 Smart Schedules 智慧助理自動排程」？

助理建立的行程，說明欄開頭會寫這一行，加上你當時說的話，讓你知道行程是怎麼來的。能看到這個行程的人（包括邀請對象）也看得到。你可以在 Google 日曆裡編輯說明，把這一行刪掉。

Contact us

For questions, suggestions or privacy requests, email support@smart-schedules.com.

To help us help you faster, please include:

  • your iPhone model and iOS version;
  • the Smart Schedules version (Settings › About › Version);
  • what happened, what you expected, and the steps to make it happen again (screenshots help).

We can't see your calendar, your chat history or anything else on your device, so please describe the problem in words. Never send passwords, verification codes or sign-in tokens; we will never ask for them.

When we reply

We read every message and usually reply within 3 business days. Privacy requests (please put "Privacy" in the subject) are handled within the time the law requires.

To open Settings, tap ☰ at the top left › General.

How do I delete my data?

Go to Settings › Data › Erase all data on this device. This:

  • stops Live Activities and removes reminder notifications;
  • withdraws AI data sharing;
  • asks Google to revoke access (this needs an internet connection) and deletes every sign-in token;
  • deletes accounts, cached events, pending changes, settings, chat history and exports.

Events in Google Calendar are not deleted; to delete the events themselves, delete them in Google Calendar. The little technical data left on our server holds no event content, and most of it expires within days; a subscription record is deleted 30 days after the latest expiry date our server has received (see How long we keep data in the Privacy Policy). Erasing data does not cancel a subscription; see the subscription answer below.

If you plan to delete the app, erase your data first, while you're online: deleting the app does not revoke Google access, and iOS may keep sign-in tokens. Afterwards you can check under Third-party apps & services in your Google Account that Smart Schedules is no longer listed.

How do I remove a Google account?

Tap your avatar at the top right, swipe left on the account under "Google accounts" (or tap ⋯), and choose Remove account. You can also tap the ⋯ next to the account's email in the ☰ menu. Removing an account asks Google to revoke the app's access and deletes that account's sign-in tokens and cached events from this iPhone; nothing in Google Calendar is deleted. If some changes haven't reached Google yet, the app tells you first and offers "Sync first".

Revoking needs an internet connection. If you were offline when you removed the account, or want to make sure access is revoked, remove Smart Schedules under Third-party apps & services in your Google Account.

How do I stop AI data sharing (withdraw consent)?

Go to Settings › AI & privacy › Stop sharing. The assistant then sends nothing more to Google Gemini. The Dynamic Island and Live Activities are not affected: they are created on your iPhone and never needed AI data sharing. Demo mode keeps working, with offline rules instead of the AI. To turn sharing back on, tap Allow on the same page. "What is sent" on that page lists the kinds of data sent (for example "Your calendar names and assistant settings, but not your Google account email addresses"); for the full list of what is sent, see section 4 of the Privacy Policy.

When does an event appear in the Dynamic Island?

Each timed event appears in the Dynamic Island and on the Lock Screen the time you chose before it starts (Settings › Notifications › Dynamic Island › Show next event: 30, 15, 10 or 5 minutes; 30 by default) and goes away after it ends. Live Activities are created on your iPhone: they need no AI data sharing, and your events are not sent to our server for them.

  • iOS 26 and later: each time you open Smart Schedules, it schedules the next two days' Live Activities, and each one appears on time even when the app isn't open.
  • iOS 18: iOS 18 doesn't let apps schedule them ahead, so an event appears only if you open Smart Schedules within that time before it starts or while it's under way. Once it appears, it stays after you close the app.

If an event never appears, check that "Show next event" isn't Off, and that Live Activities are turned on in iPhone Settings › Apps › Smart Schedules. For details, see section 5 of the Privacy Policy.

Are there in-app purchases or subscriptions?

Yes. The AI assistant has a small free monthly allowance of text requests (typed, or spoken). An auto-renewable subscription, bought through Apple in the app, gives a larger monthly allowance and lets you attach photos and PDFs; there is a free trial with a smaller allowance. The app shows the price and what is included before you subscribe.

  • Apple handles payment; we never receive your payment details or your Apple Account name or email.
  • To cancel: iPhone Settings › your name › Subscriptions. Deleting the app or erasing data does not cancel the subscription.
  • For a subscription, our server keeps only the original transaction ID Apple assigns to it, the expiry date and the number of AI requests this month (and in the trial), and deletes them 30 days after the latest expiry date it has received; for the free allowance it only counts the month's requests in the installation's App Attest record. See section 7 of the Privacy Policy.

What does the AI assistant need to work?

  • An iPhone with iOS 18 or later and an internet connection.
  • AI data sharing allowed (in the dialog the first time you use it, or Settings › AI & privacy › Allow).
  • A connected Google account, so the assistant can read and write your calendar. Without signing in, demo mode uses fictional sample calendars; allow AI data sharing to try the real assistant on them, otherwise it uses offline rules.
  • You must be 18 or older: Smart Schedules is only for people aged 18 or older (rated 18+ on the App Store), as Google's Gemini API terms require.
  • Allowance left for the month: free users have a small monthly allowance, and the subscription gives more (see the subscription answer above).
  • To speak instead of typing: tap the microphone button in the chat or Quick Capture and allow Speech Recognition and the microphone when iOS asks. Your speech is turned into text on your iPhone, and the audio is not recorded or sent anywhere; nothing is sent until you tap Send. If there is no microphone button, this iPhone can't recognise that language on the device yet; the keyboard's dictation key and Siri still work.
  • To attach a photo or PDF: the subscription. Take Photo asks for Camera permission; choosing a photo in the photo picker or a PDF from Files needs no permission, and the app receives only the one you pick. Events found in it are shown for you to review and added only when you tap Add.

For what the assistant sends, see The AI assistant in the Privacy Policy.

How do I export my data?

Go to Settings › Data › Export my data. The app creates a JSON file with your settings, what the assistant remembers, accounts and calendars, cached events and chat history, and you choose where to save or send it from the share sheet. The file never contains sign-in tokens. Photos and PDFs you attached are not kept, so they are not in the export either.

Why do my Google Calendar events say "由 Smart Schedules 智慧助理自動排程"?

Events the assistant creates begin their description with this line ("scheduled automatically by the Smart Schedules assistant") followed by what you said, so you can see where the event came from. Anyone who can see the event, including guests, can read it. You can edit the description in Google Calendar to remove the line.
