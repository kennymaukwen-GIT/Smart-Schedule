Smart Schedules

搭配 Google 日曆的 iPhone AI 行程助理

一句話就排好行程。Smart Schedules 是一款 iPhone App（iOS 18 以上）：從控制中心打開快速輸入，輸入或說出「明天下午兩點和 Joe 開線上會議」，助理就把行程加進你的 Google 日曆。

功能

  • 用日常語言新增、移動或刪除行程。長期目標（例如「半年內變壯」）會拆成每週的計畫，先給你看過，按「加入」才寫進行事曆。
  • 用說的也可以：麥克風按鈕在你的 iPhone 上把中文或英文轉成文字，聲音不會離開 iPhone。
  • 月、週、日檢視，可以連接多個 Google 帳號，每個行事曆分別顯示或隱藏。
  • 行程開始前的提醒和每日摘要；長按提醒就能直接回覆、延後或改時間。
  • 動態島和即時動態倒數、Siri 與捷徑，以及控制中心和鎖定畫面的控制項目。
  • 不登入先試用：示範模式用虛構的範例行事曆，不需要帳號，也不會用到你的 Google 帳號。允許 AI 資料分享後，可以在範例行事曆上試用真正的 AI 助理（會傳送你輸入或說出的內容和範例行程）；沒有允許或連不上時改用離線規則。

為什麼需要存取你的 Google 日曆

Smart Schedules 是你 Google 日曆的用戶端。它讀取你的行程，用來顯示月曆、提醒和即時動態；在你（或你要求助理）新增、修改、刪除行程時寫回 Google 日曆；並讀取你的行事曆清單，讓你選擇要顯示哪些行事曆、新行程存到哪裡。與 Google 日曆的同步直接在你的 iPhone 和 Google 之間進行；只有你允許 AI 資料分享後，相關行程才會經過我們的伺服器送到 Gemini。動態島和即時動態直接在你的 iPhone 上產生，不會上傳行程。

AI 與隱私

助理用 Google 的 Gemini API 理解你的話。點「允許」之前，不會傳送任何資料給 AI；允許後，你的請求和相關的行程會經過我們的伺服器（在 Cloudflare 上執行）直接轉送到 Gemini，伺服器不保存任何行事曆資料，你也可以隨時在 App 裡停止分享。用麥克風按鈕說的話只在 iPhone 上轉成文字，App 只傳送文字，從不傳送聲音。App 附上的行事曆資訊不包含你 Google 帳號的 email 地址：有好幾個帳號時，助理只看到「Account 1」「Account 2」這類代稱。依 Gemini API 的條款，Smart Schedules 只提供給年滿 18 歲的人使用。沒有廣告、沒有追蹤，我們不出售資料。我們不會用 Google 使用者資料來開發、改進或訓練任何 AI 或機器學習模型。完整說明請看隱私權政策。

Smart Schedules 使用從 Google API 取得的資訊，以及將這些資訊傳輸到任何其他 App，都將遵守 Google API 服務使用者資料政策（Google API Services User Data Policy），包括其中的「有限使用」（Limited Use）規定。

Smart Schedules

An AI scheduling assistant for iPhone that works with your Google Calendar

Plan your day in a sentence. Smart Schedules is an iPhone app (iOS 18 or later): open Quick Capture from Control Center, type or say "online meeting with Joe tomorrow at 2 pm", and the assistant adds it to your Google Calendar.

Features

  • Add, move or delete events in everyday language. Long-term goals (such as "get stronger in six months") become a weekly plan that you review first; nothing is added until you tap Add.
  • Speak instead of typing: the microphone button turns Chinese or English speech into text on your iPhone, and the audio never leaves it.
  • Month, week and day views, with several Google accounts and each calendar shown or hidden as you like.
  • Reminders before events and a daily briefing; long-press a reminder to reply, snooze or reschedule right there.
  • Dynamic Island and Live Activity countdowns, Siri and Shortcuts, and a Control Center and Lock Screen control.
  • Try without signing in: demo mode has fictional sample calendars, needs no account and never uses your Google account. Allow AI data sharing to try the real AI assistant on them (it sends what you type or say and the sample events); without permission or a connection, it uses offline rules.

Why Smart Schedules uses your Google Calendar

Smart Schedules is a client for your Google Calendar. It reads your events to show your calendar, reminders and Live Activities; writes to Google Calendar when you (or the assistant, at your request) add, change or delete events; and reads your list of calendars so you can choose which ones to show and where new events go. Syncing with Google Calendar happens directly between your iPhone and Google. Only after you allow AI data sharing do the related events go through our server to Gemini. The Dynamic Island and Live Activities are created on your iPhone, with no events uploaded.

AI and privacy

The assistant uses Google's Gemini API to understand what you say. Nothing is sent to the AI until you tap Allow. After that, your request and the related events go through our server (running on Cloudflare), which passes them straight to Gemini and stores none of your calendar data; you can stop sharing in the app at any time. What you say with the microphone button is turned into text on your iPhone; the app sends only the text, never audio. The calendar details the app adds never include your Google account email addresses: with several accounts, the assistant sees only names such as "Account 1" and "Account 2". Under the Gemini API terms, Smart Schedules is only for people aged 18 or older. No ads, no tracking, and we never sell data. We never use Google user data to develop, improve or train any AI or machine-learning models. The Privacy Policy explains everything in detail.

Smart Schedules' use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
